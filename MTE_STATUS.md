## CET-KG PROOF-OF-CONCEPT

Completed.

A synthetic proof-of-concept demonstrates the core research direction:

- Temporal event-based forecasting
- Forecast uncertainty estimation
- Conformal-style prediction-set calibration
- Graph-grounded explanation
- Evaluation-oriented result generation

The PoC is intended to demonstrate feasibility of the proposed approach and is not presented as final benchmark validation.

## CET-KG FINAL BENCHMARK EXPERIMENTS

Planned evaluation includes:

- MRR and Hits@K

- Explanation fidelity and sparsity
- Robustness under historical and supervision noise
- Comparison with relevant baseline methods
